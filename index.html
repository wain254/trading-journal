The BTMM Framework

How the session actually moves

Beat The Market Maker reads price as a sequence of engineered moves across three sessions — not a random walk. Every setup is timed to where liquidity sits, not to an indicator crossing a line.

00:00 – 07:00 GMT

Asian Range

Price coils inside a tight range as the market maker builds a book. This range marks the liquidity pools — the highs and lows — that London will come to take.

07:00 – 10:00 GMT

The Judas Swing

London opens with a false move through the Asian range to trigger retail stops and fill institutional orders, before reversing hard in the true direction.

12:00 – 16:00 GMT

New York Reversal

New York confirms or extends the London reversal. This is where SilverBullet FX looks for the highest-probability continuation entry on the 3-EMA structure.

Instruments

XAU/USD — GOLD

Gold

Deep liquidity and clean session structure make gold the primary BTMM instrument — the Judas swing shows up here with unusual reliability around London open.

  • Strong reaction to session highs/lows
  • Clear 3-EMA (13/50/200) structure on M15/H1
  • High volume around London & NY overlap
XAG/USD — SILVER

Silver

Faster and thinner than gold, silver extends further once a Judas swing confirms — useful for scaling size on the desk's highest-conviction setups.

  • Larger point moves per session
  • Correlates with gold structure, adds confluence
  • Reserved for confirmed, high-conviction setups
The Edge

What we actually look for

No signal fires without all four of these lining up. That's deliberate — fewer trades, higher conviction.

◆Liquidity Engineering

Mapping session highs and lows before they're taken, so a stop-hunt reads as an entry signal instead of a losing trade.

◆3-EMA Structure

13/50/200 EMA stack on the entry timeframe confirms trend direction before any Judas swing is traded.

◆Stop-Hunt Recognition

Reading the false break itself — speed, wick size, and session timing — rather than reacting after the reversal is obvious.

◆Session Timing

Every entry is scheduled to a session window. No trades taken purely because price "looks" right outside that window.
