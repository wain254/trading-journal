## 📱 **Features**

### 🔐 **User System**
- ✅ Unique user accounts (no two users can share username)
- ✅ Secure password hashing
- ✅ Password hide/unhide toggle (eye icon)
- ✅ Cloud-based accounts (works on ANY device)

### 📊 **Trade Management**
- ✅ Log trades with entry/exit prices
- ✅ Track position size and P&L
- ✅ 6 built-in consistency rules
- ✅ Real-time adherence percentage
- ✅ Delete individual trades

### 📅 **Calendar Analytics**
- ✅ Monthly calendar view
- ✅ Daily P&L color-coded (green/red)
- ✅ Trade count per day
- ✅ Daily win rate display
- ✅ Click any day for detailed breakdown

### 📈 **Dashboard Statistics**
- ✅ Total trades count
- ✅ Average consistency score
- ✅ Overall win rate
- ✅ Total P&L
- ✅ Consistency streak (consecutive 100% rule adherence)
- ✅ Best trading day ever

### 🧮 **Built-in Calculators**
- ✅ Position Size Calculator (Risk % based)
- ✅ Pip Value Calculator
- ✅ Risk Management tools

### ☁️ **Cloud Backup**
- ✅ Automatic cloud backup to Google Sheets
- ✅ Cross-device synchronization
- ✅ Restore trades from any device
- ✅ Zero setup for users

### 📚 **Resources Section**
- ✅ Free Forex eBooks links
- ✅ Preferred Broker integration (OFinancial Markets)
- ✅ Trader reviews/testimonials
- ✅ WhatsApp support contact

### 🎨 **Design Features**
- ✅ Glassmorphism UI design
- ✅ Smooth hover animations
- ✅ Responsive layout (mobile + desktop)
- ✅ Gradient backgrounds
- ✅ Professional color scheme
